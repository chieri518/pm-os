# Notice

## On sources and originality

All content in this repository is original writing. Nothing here reproduces text
from any book, course, or commercial preparation product.

Where the content covers established ideas — Hick's Law, the Kano model, Jobs to
Be Done, prospect theory, MECE — those ideas are attributed to their originators
in a `sources` field on the relevant node, and the surrounding explanation is
written from scratch.

Several published works informed **what topics to cover**. Chief among them is
*Cracking the PM Interview* by Gayle Laakmann McDowell and Jackie Bavaro, which
remains the standard text and the better linear read. This project is a
complement to it, not a substitute or a derivative: it is organised as a graph
you traverse rather than chapters you read, it adds when *not* to use each
framework, and it checks the underlying research and flags where it is contested.

Example interview questions here are either widely circulated in the industry or
written for this project. They are not reproduced from any published question list.

## On accuracy

Content carries an explicit `evidence` verdict — `replicated`, `supported`,
`heuristic`, or `contested` — and a note explaining what the underlying research
does and does not support. Several claims commonly taught as settled are labelled
contested here, with citations, including:

- the 3:1 LTV:CAC target (an investor guideline for mature SaaS, not a finding)
- Net Promoter Score as a predictor of growth
- the 40% product-market-fit survey threshold
- loss aversion

Corrections are welcome. If something here is wrong, the `sources` field is the
place to start the argument.
